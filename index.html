Location & Proximity

At Toledo Circle Apartments, you are connected to the very best of Toledo, Ohio. Everything you need is right around the corner:

  • University of Toledo: Just minutes away, making walking, biking, or taking a quick shuttle ride to campus a breeze.
  • Downtown Toledo: Less than 15 minutes away, putting city dining, entertainment, and employment hubs within easy reach.
  • Ottawa Park: Spend your weekends enjoying the walking trails, tennis courts, golf course, and historic amphitheater.
  • Local Dining & Retail: Satisfy your cravings or run errands at numerous popular eateries and shops along Dorr St and Secor Rd.
  • Transit Connectivity: Easy access to TARTA public transportation lines and major roadways makes commuting stress-free.
